# Hyderabad & Regional Destinations: Master Venue Cards Dataset (Set 1)

**Project:** Hyderabad & Telangana Hyper-Local, Budget-Intelligent Trip Planner  
**Role:** Creative Director & Product Lead (NIFT Hyderabad) | Technical Project Architect (AGY)  
**Document Purpose:** Complete, factual, and verified data for individual places in and around Hyderabad, formatted strictly according to the Set 1 Venue Card architecture.

---

## Data Schema Reference for Every Venue Card
Each individual place card contains:
* **Place Name & Regional Zone**
* **Budget Tier Eligibility:** `[Easy]` (≤ ₹2,000/day), `[Premium]` (₹5,500–₹16,000/day), `[Luxury]` (₹25,000+/day)
* **Dwell Time:** Recommended duration to explore
* **Operating Hours & Best Visit Window:** Regular opening hours, closed days, and optimal time-of-day
* **Ticket Cost & Accepted Payments:** Pricing for Indian & Foreign visitors, camera fees, and payment methods (Cash, UPI, Card, Online)
* **Hygienic Stays Proximity:** Curated nearby clean accommodation radius and neighborhoods
