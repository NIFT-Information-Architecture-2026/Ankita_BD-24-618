# Master Feature Cards for Card Sorting Study

**Project:** Hyderabad & Telangana Hyper-Local Trip Planner  
**Role:** Creative Director & Product Lead (NIFT Hyderabad) | Technical Project Architect (AGY)  
**UX Research Phase:** Phase 3 — Open / Hybrid Card Sorting Study  
**Document Purpose:** The complete deck of 23 individual feature cards formatted as printable/sharable flashcards for research participants to sort, group, and label.

---

## 🎴 Master Deck: All 23 Feature Cards

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 01] Quick Login & Sign-In (< 1 Min)                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Quick, lightweight login via phone number, Google, or  │
│   Apple in under 60 seconds.                                           │
│ • User Value: Securely saves your trip plans and preferences without   │
│   wasting time on long, frustrating sign-up forms.                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 02] City Selector (India-Wide Scalable)                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Choose which city you want to explore (starts with     │
│   Hyderabad, expandable to other cities like Ranchi, Allahabad, etc.). │
│ • User Value: Clearly defines the geographic focus of your trip with   │
│   room for multi-city travel in the future.                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 03] Trip Dates & Duration Selector (Arrival to Departure)        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Select your arrival date and departure date, which     │
│   automatically calculates your total number of trip days.             │
│ • User Value: Helps the app tailor the pace, rest buffers, and number  │
│   of places across your exact duration of stay.                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 04] Traveler Type Selector                                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Choose who is traveling: Solo Wanderer, Duo / Friends, │
│   or Family Unit.                                                      │
│ • User Value: Adjusts recommendations for safety, comfort, walking     │
│   distances, rest pacing, and group dynamics.                          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 05] Two-Tier Budget Selector (Tier + Detailed Numerical Ranges)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: First, choose a high-level tier (Budget-Friendly,      │
│   Premium, or Exclusive). Then, pick a specific numerical value range  │
│   within that category (e.g., ₹1,700–₹2,000/day vs ₹2,500–₹3,500/day). │
│ • User Value: Lets you set your exact spending limits without vague    │
│   generalizations so your itinerary never crosses your budget.         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 06] Traveler Experience Level (1st Time vs. Returning Explorer)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A prompt asking: "Is this your first time in the city, │
│   or have you visited before?"                                         │
│ • User Value: Routes new visitors to an effortless automated plan, and │
│   gives experienced explorers full freedom to build their own.         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 07] Guided Automated Itinerary Generator                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Automatically builds a complete, day-by-day travel     │
│   plan matching your budget, group type, and dates with zero guesswork.│
│ • User Value: Eliminates hours of stressful research for travelers who │
│   want a reliable, cluster-smart, ready-to-go plan.                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 08] Manual Self-Planner (Build-Your-Own Itinerary)               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A customizable workspace where users who know the city │
│   can freely select destinations and build their own custom day plan.  │
│ • User Value: Gives experienced travelers total freedom and autonomy   │
│   to craft their own bespoke route at their own pace.                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 09] Budget & Group-Matched Stay Suggestions                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Displays verified clean, hygienic hotels, hostels, or  │
│   resorts filtered strictly by your chosen budget range and group type │
│   (solo rooms, twin-sharing, family suites).                           │
│ • User Value: Eliminates the headache of searching through hundreds of │
│   random, unverified hotel listings.                                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 10] Direct Official Stay Booking Links                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Provides a direct one-tap button on each stay card     │
│   linking directly to the hotel's official website or booking partner. │
│ • User Value: Allows you to book your room securely at official prices │
│   without the app taking cuts or processing payments.                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 11] Open Dashboard (Home Hub)                                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A welcoming, unhurried landing home screen where you   │
│   can jump into your current trip, start a new plan, or explore spots. │
│ • User Value: Avoids aggressive form pop-ups and provides a calm,      │
│   clear starting point every time you open the app.                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 12] Active Day Itinerary Timeline (Stop-by-Stop Schedule)        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A clean, step-by-step visual schedule for today showing│
│   morning to evening stops, dwell time at each spot, and next stop.    │
│ • User Value: Keeps you oriented on the ground so you always know      │
│   where to go next, when to move, and how to pace your day.            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 13] Skip Next Stop Button                                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A simple button to skip the upcoming scheduled place   │
│   if you are tired, running late, or enjoying your current spot.       │
│ • User Value: Keeps travel flexible and prevents schedule stress when  │
│   unexpected things happen.                                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 14] Reorder Places (To-and-Fro Shifting within the Day)          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Allows you to rearrange the order of stops within      │
│   today's plan (e.g., visit Spot B before Spot A).                     │
│ • User Value: Lets you adapt to daily preferences while keeping all    │
│   your planned spots intact.                                           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 15] Smart Swap with Same-Cluster Alternatives                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Replace any scheduled place with other verified        │
│   attractions located in the exact same neighborhood or zone.          │
│ • User Value: Lets you change a spot without messing up your route or  │
│   adding hours of cross-city transit.                                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 16] Spatial Advisory Warning Pop-Up                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A gentle alert that appears if you try to swap to a    │
│   spot that is too far across the city, warning you of travel time.    │
│ • User Value: Protects you from accidentally creating unrealistic,     │
│   exhausting routes across city traffic while keeping choice yours.    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 17] Real-Time "Open / Closed" Alerts                             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: Clear badges marking whether a venue is currently open,│
│   closed today (e.g., Fridays/Mondays), or approaching closing time.   │
│ • User Value: Guarantees you never arrive at a museum or palace only   │
│   to find the gates locked.                                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 18] Contextual Nearby Meal Radar Page (Live API Nearby Dining)   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A dedicated dining page accessible after any stop that │
│   uses live API data to show open cafes/restaurants within walking     │
│   distance matching your budget.                                       │
│ • User Value: Solves the "Where should we eat right now?" dilemma      │
│   without forcing rigid restaurants into your schedule.                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 19] Live Location Tracking Toggle (Safety)                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: An optional, user-controlled toggle to enable live     │
│   tracking and trip monitoring, designed for solo & women travelers.   │
│ • User Value: Provides peace of mind and personal safety without       │
│   forceful tracking when you don't want it.                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 20] Emergency SOS & Local Helplines                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A one-tap safety button with instant dialing for Police│
│   (112), Women's Helpline (1091), and live location sharing with family│
│ • User Value: Provides instant, accessible safety support if any       │
│   uncomfortable situation arises.                                      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 21] Offline Saved Day Pass                                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A one-tap button to save or download today's complete  │
│   timeline, addresses, and ticket info to your phone.                  │
│ • User Value: Ensures you never lose your schedule even if mobile data │
│   drops in dense market alleys, underground forts, or hills.           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 22] Places Directory & Search                                    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A searchable catalog of all 45 verified Hyderabad     │
│   attractions, viewpoints, parks, and monuments with factual info.     │
│ • User Value: Allows you to browse, search, and discover places        │
│   independently at your own leisure.                                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [CARD 23] Saved Trips & Bookmarks Archive                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What it does: A personal archive storing all your generated trip     │
│   plans, past itineraries, and bookmarked favorite places.             │
│ • User Value: Keeps all your travel research in one organized place    │
│   for future reference.                                                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
