## 5. Algorithmic Rules for Itinerary Assembly (Phase 4 Logic)

When generating a daily itinerary in Phase 4, the engine applies these 4 rules simultaneously:
1. **Rule 1 (Spatial Constraint):** All destinations for Day X must belong to the **same Spatial Cluster** (Dimension 1).
2. **Rule 2 (Operational Check):** No Friday booking for Chowmahalla/Salar Jung; no Monday booking for Nehru Zoo; no Wednesday booking for Statue of Equality (Dimension 2).
3. **Rule 3 (Budget Constraint):** Easy Trip selects Tier 1 & Tier 2; Premium Trip selects Tier 1, 2 & 3; Luxury selects Tier 1, 2, 3 & 4 (Dimension 3).
4. **Rule 4 (Dwell Buffer):** Max daily dwell time ≤ 6.5 hours (including 1 Category 4C anchor + 1 Category 4B anchor + 1 Category 4A stop + 2 hours for lunch/rest) (Dimension 4).
