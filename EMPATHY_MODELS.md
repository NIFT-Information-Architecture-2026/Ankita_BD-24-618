## 2. Detailed Persona Profiles

### Persona A: The Solo Wanderer
* **Name & Archetype:** Riya, 22 | Fashion Communication Student & Solo Cultural Traveler
* **Goals:** Explore authentic neighborhood lanes, discover hidden cafes, photograph architectural heritage, stay within a tight budget (₹1,000–₹1,500/day).
* **Pain Points & Fears:** 
  * Fear of getting scammed or stranded in unfamiliar areas.
  * Overwhelm when trying to decide where to eat near a site.
  * Losing data signal in dense market alleys.
* **Key App Requirement:** High walkability clarity, offline itinerary card, budget-friendly nearby cafe recommendations.

---

### Persona B: The Duo / Friends & Couples
* **Name & Archetype:** Aarav & Kabir, 24 | Young Professionals on a Weekend City Escape
* **Goals:** Balanced mix of heritage trails (Golconda/Charminar), modern nightlife/cafes (Jubilee Hills), aesthetic photo spots, mid-range comfort.
* **Pain Points & Fears:**
  * Exhausting cross-city travel that wastes their short 2-day weekend.
  * Arriving at iconic places only to find long ticket lines or closed gates.
* **Key App Requirement:** Zone-based daily clustering (one area per day), accurate venue operating hours, mid-range dining spots.

---

### Persona C: The Family Unit
* **Name & Archetype:** The Verma Family (Parents + 2 Kids/Elderly Parent)
* **Goals:** Smooth, stress-free exploration of top Hyderabad sights and parks, predictable meal times, reliable hygiene and rest stops.
* **Pain Points & Fears:**
  * Over-ambitious itineraries that cause physical exhaustion for kids/elders.
  * Unexpected delays, missing lunch spots, or crowded afternoon heat.
* **Key App Requirement:** Afternoon rest window integration, predictable dwell times, spacious family-friendly dining recommendations.

## 3. Empathy Maps

### Persona A: Solo Wanderer (Riya)

| Category | Insights |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SAYS** | *"I want to explore the real local culture, but I don't want to get scammed or end up in a sketchy street at night."* |
| **THINKS** | *"Is this museum actually open today? Will I have time to eat lunch before my next stop?"* |
| **DOES** | Constantly checks phone for maps, takes photos, looks for budget street food and quiet heritage cafes. |
| **FEELS** | Excited by discovery; anxious about logistics, safety, and overspending. |

---

### Persona B: Duo / Friends (Aarav & Kabir)

| Category | Insights |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SAYS** | *"Let's cover the Old City in the morning, grab good food nearby, and hit Jubilee Hills cafes by evening."* |
| **THINKS** | *"Hope we aren't spending half our day sitting in Hyderabad traffic."* |
| **DOES** | Takes autos/metro, snaps aesthetic photos, looks for vibrant places to hang out. |
| **FEELS** | Eager to maximize time; frustrated by inefficient routes or closed venues. |

---

### Persona C: Family Unit (The Verma Family)

| Category | Insights |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SAYS** | *"We need a clear plan for the day so the kids don't get tired and hungry."* |
| **THINKS** | *"Where can we rest between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM when it's hot outside?"* |
| **DOES** | Moves together as a group, searches for comfortable seating, clear entry prices, and clean food stops. |
| **FEELS** | Protective, seeking comfort and predictability; stressed by sudden delays. |
